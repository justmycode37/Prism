# Prospective research contract

Prism discovers long-only daily equity hypotheses in AI infrastructure. Historical prices, today's universe, news and model knowledge are development inputs. Historical screens do not establish untouched out-of-sample performance. Every candidate is frozen in an immutable registry before future observations can count.

The initial families are momentum, mean reversion, breakout and structured event surprise. Event surprise requires a dated numeric actual and a prior dated expectation; narrative confidence is not a feature. A hypothesis states its mechanism, prediction horizon, supporting and conflicting evidence, and invalidation conditions. Arbitrary generated code is not executable.

One versioned policy defines session signals, next-open scheduling, fills, costs, stops, holding sessions, allocation and portfolio limits for replay and forward simulation. The data contract supplies observed exchange sessions, raw prices and corporate actions. Missing execution metadata prevents a fill. Daily data does not justify imaginary intraday stop fills.

Each candidate runs in an independent paper shadow account. Historical rejection remains visible and does not become a claim of forward success. Promotion to the aggregate paper account requires registered future sessions, completed trades, independent blocks, positive operating returns and benchmark excess, acceptable drawdown and uncertainty at predeclared review checkpoints. Changing the rule or policy creates a new experiment; changing a display ID cannot retrade a consumed economic event.

Reports compare the stock's buy-and-hold, exposure-matched holdings, available sector/market data, and a simple technical baseline. Missing baselines and survivorship-safe data are explicit coverage gaps. Trading costs and research/data/infrastructure costs are reported separately and together. Passing engineering checks does not establish a profitable strategy.

The 33-role roster remains available. Defaults activate six collectors and two analysts; deterministic selection and authorization avoid unnecessary strategist/executor model calls. Public acquisition precedes model work. Unchanged daily histories and source content reuse their prior research. Material new evidence or a changed policy triggers a new bounded cycle.

Development subagents default to GPT-6 Luna. Runtime model routing remains separately configured. The installed service stays paused during implementation and all verification uses offline data.
